Math Wheel Challenge

How to Play

1. Click Spin Wheel to rotate the wheel.

2. The number at the bottom pointer is your dividend.

3. The number at the center is divisor.

4. Enter the answer in the input box and click on OK.

5. Get all 8 answers correct for a special celebration!

Example: If center number is 12 and selected number is 36, the answer would be 3 (36 ÷ 12 = 3)
